| Slot | Keyboard | Controller | Action | Function |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | RT+A | Basic Synthesis | Progress |
| 2 | 2 | RT+B | Basic Touch | Quality |
| 3 | 3 | RT+Y | Master's Mend | Durability recovery |
| 4 | 4 | RT+D-Up | Standard Touch | Quality combo |
| 5 | Shift+1 | RT+X | Veneration | Progress buff |
| 6 | Shift+2 | RT+D-Right | Innovation | Quality buff |
| 7 | Shift+3 | RT+D-Down | Waste Not II | Durability efficiency |
| 8 | Shift+4 | RT+D-Left | Great Strides | Next-touch buff |
| 9 | Shift+= | LT+D-Up | Byregot's Blessing | Quality finisher |
| 10 | Shift+- | LT+D-Left | Manipulation | Durability regeneration |
| 11 | Shift+5 | LT+D-Right | Muscle Memory | Progress opener |
| 12 | Shift+6 | LT+D-Down | Reflect | Quality opener |

## Usage

Choose Muscle Memory for a progress opener or Reflect for a quality opener, only on the first step. Use Veneration with synthesis, Innovation with touches, and Basic Touch then Standard Touch for their CP combo. Manage durability with Waste Not II, Manipulation and repair; build Inner Quiet before Great Strides into Byregot. Finish progress before durability runs out. All actions are manual and require their unlocks; this is a compact general layout, not an expert-craft rotation.

## Usage

Use yield and boon buffs to suit the node and available GP. Use the integrity button for Solid Reason/Ageless Words; Wrath offers Wise to the World during Eureka Moment. For collectables, enable Collect, then build collectability with Scrutiny and Scour or Meticulous before collecting. The search button first offers Truth when its buff is absent, then the rare-node search. Enable Prospect/Triangulate and Sneak through Actions & Traits when needed.

| 5 | Shift+1 | RT+X | Snagging | Special catches / Salvage |
| 6 | Shift+2 | RT+D-Right | Fish Eyes | Time restriction / Vital Sight |

| 8 | Shift+4 | RT+D-Left | Cast Light | Light / Electric Current |
| 9 | Shift+= | LT+D-Up | Patience II | Large-catch chance |
| 10 | Shift+- | LT+D-Left | Precision Hookset | Weak-bite hookset |
| 11 | Shift+5 | LT+D-Right | Powerful Hookset | Strong-bite hookset |
| 12 | Shift+6 | LT+D-Down | Thaliak's Favor | GP recovery |

## Usage

Select bait and enable Collect through Actions & Traits before fishing when needed. Cast changes to Hook while fishing and Gig while diving. Under Patience use Precision Hookset for weak bites and Powerful Hookset for strong bites. Mooch, Surface Slap, Prize Catch, Snagging, Fish Eyes, Chum and Cast Light change to their listed spearfishing functions while diving. Use Thaliak’s Favor to recover GP when available. Bait, catch choice and every activation remain manual; specialized ocean-fishing and big-fish sequences need omitted actions.
